# CalculationTime Flooring Calculator Link

## Decision

Add the CalculationTime professional flooring calculator link to the Turner Installs Floor Preparation service page:

`app/services/floor-preparation/page.tsx`

Target URL:

`https://www.calculationtime.com/calculators/professional-flooring-calculator/`

## Why This Placement

The floor-preparation page is the strongest contextual fit because it already discusses the real job that comes before quoting and installation: laser checks, substrate condition, levelling, moisture, tools, materials, and cost uncertainty.

The calculator is useful at the point where a homeowner or builder is thinking about floor area, material quantity, waste allowance, pack coverage, and planning assumptions. That makes the link helpful rather than a random outbound footer link.

## Link Treatment

The link is presented as a planning tool, not as Turner Installs' final quoting mechanism. The copy makes clear that the calculator is for early estimating and that a Turner Installs site measure/final quote is still needed.

## Future Options

- Add a shorter footer utility link if CalculationTime should receive sitewide discovery.
- Add contextual links from floor-cost blog content if the blog pages become a major SEO entry point.
- Add UTM tracking later if analytics attribution becomes important.
