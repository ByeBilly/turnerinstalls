# CalculationTime Flooring Calculator Link

## Decision

Add the CalculationTime professional flooring calculator link to the Turner Installs Floor Preparation service page and footer brand area:

`app/services/floor-preparation/page.tsx`
`components/Footer.tsx`

Target URL:

`https://www.calculationtime.com/calculators/professional-flooring-calculator/`

## Why This Placement

The floor-preparation page is the strongest contextual fit because it already discusses the real job that comes before quoting and installation: laser checks, substrate condition, levelling, moisture, tools, materials, and cost uncertainty.

The footer placement matches Billy's requested visual location: directly under the Turner Installs logo/tagline area shown at the bottom of the homepage. It gives the calculator a visible utility placement without interrupting quote/contact conversion areas.

The footer anchor text now uses a citation-style sentence:

> We use this free flooring calculator from CalculationTime.com because it works.

Billy confirmed this footer card is the optimal Turner Installs placement/copy on 2026-06-12.

The calculator is useful at the point where a homeowner or builder is thinking about floor area, material quantity, waste allowance, pack coverage, and planning assumptions. That makes the link helpful rather than a random outbound footer link.

## Link Treatment

The link is presented as a planning tool, not as Turner Installs' final quoting mechanism. The copy makes clear that the calculator is for early estimating and that a Turner Installs site measure/final quote is still needed.

The footer link uses UTM parameters for clearer cross-site attribution:

`https://www.calculationtime.com/calculators/professional-flooring-calculator/?utm_source=turnerinstalls&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=footer_flooring_planning_tool`
