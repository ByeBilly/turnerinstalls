## What Turner Installs Does

Turner Installs is a specialist flooring contractor with 15+ years of experience
and third-generation trade knowledge. The business operates across the residential
and commercial sectors, with a strong emphasis on professional floor preparation
as the foundation of every project.

### Core Services

1. **Vinyl Plank Installation**
   High-quality floating vinyl plank flooring, installed with precision and clean
   transitions. Suitable for residential and commercial environments.

2. **Hybrid Flooring Installation**
   Premium hybrid planks installed for durability and aesthetics. Ideal for
   high-traffic areas, wet zones, and modern interiors.

3. **Timber Flooring Installation**
   Engineered and solid timber installation for classic and high-end residential
   projects.

4. **Laminate Flooring Installation**
   Cost-effective laminate options installed to manufacturer specifications.

5. **Professional Floor Preparation (Flood Levelling)**
   The signature service. Turner Installs is renowned for delivering mirror-flat
   subfloors using professional-grade floor levelling compounds and techniques.
   This step eliminates bouncing, hollow spots, and unevenness that cause
   premature floor failure. This is the most important differentiator of the
   Turner Installs service.

6. **Floor Removal & Clean Demolition**
   Safe and clean removal of existing flooring prior to new installation.

7. **Commercial Flooring**
   Office fit-outs, retail spaces, and multi-site commercial projects. Delivered
   on schedule and strictly to code.
