## Domain And Email (Correcting The Gemini Advice)

- `turnerinstalls.com` has Google Workspace MX records; `turnerinstalls.com.au`
  has **none**. Liam's mailbox genuinely lives on `.com`. Switching the site to
  `liam@turnerinstalls.com.au` as Gemini suggested would send customer email
  into a void. Keep `.com` for email. To use `.com.au` later, add it as an
  alias domain in Google Workspace first, test, then change `BUSINESS.email`.
- The `.com` is not a problem for Google, but it is wasted right now:
  `http://turnerinstalls.com` returns a Google 404 and HTTPS does not answer.
  **Action:** add `turnerinstalls.com` and `www.turnerinstalls.com` to the
  Vercel project as redirect domains (308 to `https://www.turnerinstalls.com.au`).
  Only change the A/CNAME web records; leave the MX records alone.
- `https://turnerinstalls.com.au` redirects to `www` with a **307 (temporary)**.
  **Action:** in Vercel > Domains, set the apex redirect to 308 (permanent).

## What Only You Or Liam Can Do (Highest Impact First)

1. **Fill `recentJobs` on the top 10-15 suburbs.** Each suburb entry in
   `data/flooringInstallationSuburbs.ts` / `data/suburbs.ts` now accepts:
   ```ts
   recentJobs: [{
     title: "Carpet uplift + hybrid install, 3-bed lowset",
     summary: "Old carpet and smoothedge out, slab ground where the old tiles had been, two low spots self-levelled, 85 m² hybrid laid.",
     date: "2026-08",
     services: ["Carpet uplift", "Concrete grinding", "Self-levelling"],
     image: { src: "/installspics/...", alt: "Hybrid floor after slab levelling in Sherwood" },
   }]
   ```
   The section stays hidden until there is data, so nothing fake is shown.
   Two real jobs per suburb is the single biggest fix for the template problem.
2. **Reviews.** Only 4 reviews in `data/reviews.json`. Send the
   `GOOGLE_REVIEW_URL` by SMS within a day of every handover, and ask
   customers to mention the suburb and the job ("floor levelling in Sherwood").
3. **Google Business Profile.** Categories: Flooring contractor (primary),
   Concrete contractor. Services list matching the site's service pages.
   Service areas matching `BUSINESS.areaServed`. Post job photos weekly.
4. **`sameAs` links.** Add the GBP URL, Facebook, Instagram and any directory
   listings to `BUSINESS.sameAs` in `lib/business.ts`. This is how AI engines
   confirm the site and the Google listing are the same business.
5. **Citations with identical NAP** ("Turner Installs", 0413 592 054, Oxley
   QLD 4074): Hipages, Yellow Pages, True Local, Houzz, Oneflare, ServiceSeeking.
6. **Search Console.** Check Page indexing > "Crawled - currently not indexed".
   If many suburb pages sit there after 4-6 weeks, add real jobs to them or
   noindex the weakest ones. Do not delete them blind.
7. **Problem-first guides** (blog posts are 485-634 words): "Why is my hybrid
   floor bouncing?", "How much does floor levelling cost in Brisbane?",
